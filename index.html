About me

I am currently a PhD student, studying Computer Science at UBC, supervised by Leonid Sigal and Robert Xiao.
My passion broadly lies in artificial intelligence, computer vision, and machine learning. What I am really interested in is analyzing images and videos to apply my knowledege in machine learnin and artificial intelligence into practical applications.

Currently I am focusing on facilitating building tools to facilitate using AI generative models. To see the detailed information about my experiences, projects and publications please see my CV.

I completed my M.Sc. studies jointly in Vision Science and System Design Engineering at the University of Waterloo, under the supervision of Vasudevan Lakshminarayan and John Zelek. I was as also part of the VIP lab.
