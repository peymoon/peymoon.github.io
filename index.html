Peyman Gholami

I'm a final-year PhD candidate in Computer Science at the University of British Columbia, advised by Leonid Sigal and Robert Xiao. My research spans Artificial Intelligence, Human-Computer Interaction, and the full spectrum between these two. What I am really interested in is applying AI and multi-modal learning techniques to interactive and creative applications.

My PhD work focuses on Selective Intervention for Efficient and Controllable Visual Foundation Models, which addresses the shortcomings that limit these models in practice by finding where targeted changes make the biggest difference. Along the way, I've had the chance to work closely with leading researchers at Google DeepMind, Meta and Microsoft Research. Before that I was a research scientist at Mirametrix, building face and gaze tracking systems, and did my M.Sc. at the University of Waterloo.

I'm on the job market for research positions starting in early 2027.

Selected publications

DISCOVER: Online Variance-Guided Data Discovery for Budgeted Multimodal GRPO
NeurIPS, 2026
project page / paper

Decides which prompts are worth labeling while a vision-language model trains, focusing on the ones it still answers inconsistently. With only 10% of the data labeled, it outperforms GRPO trained on the full pool for the same number of steps.

Layered Diffusion Brushes
ICCV, 2025  (Best Demo Award)
project page / demo / arXiv / video

Brush-based image editing with diffusion models, where every edit lives on its own layer and can be hidden, adjusted or removed later. No training required, and edits appear in real time.
